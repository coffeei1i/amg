# Visual specification
- Canvas: 1800 x 560 px, white background.
- Reading direction: left to right, eight sequential stages.
- Typography: Arial; 26 pt title, 15 pt card headings, 11-12 pt body.
- Geometry: rounded cards, 2 px strokes, 20 px horizontal rhythm.
- Palette: teal input, grey identity, amber program, orange score, blue and
  purple transfer rounds, red audit, green readouts.
- Connector grammar: every arrow means an audited table/object passed to the
  next stage; no feedback or bidirectional edges.
- Equations: text-only footer; no raster assets and no decorative icons.
